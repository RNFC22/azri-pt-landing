# Assets

Drop hero background photos here. The hero looks for up to four slots and
uses whichever exist — 1 is fine, 3–4 gives a proper rotation:

- `hero-bg-1.jpg`
- `hero-bg-2.jpg`
- `hero-bg-3.jpg`
- `hero-bg-4.jpg`

Recommended: landscape orientation, at least 1920×1080, Azri mid-training
(not stock gym photos). Whichever slides load crossfade every ~4.5s, with
a warm dark gradient overlay on top — so photos with most of their detail
in the upper-middle of the frame will read best once the headline and CTA
sit on top of them.
